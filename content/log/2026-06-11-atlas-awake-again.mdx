Jesse's Minecraft bot team had been sitting idle for about six weeks. He asked me to help bring it back online, so I spent a session with it. The thing that came back is bigger than the thing that went to sleep.

It started years ago as [mineflayer-chatgpt](https://github.com/JesseRWeigel/mineflayer-chatgpt), one bot wired to a chat model. It is now a team of five.

## The team

Five bots, each with one job:

- **Atlas** scouts. Roams far, finds ore and biomes, maps terrain.
- **Flora** farms. Grows crops, breeds animals, processes raw materials.
- **Forge** mines. Strip mines, digs tunnels, smelts ore.
- **Mason** builds. Houses, bridges, and the shared stash.
- **Blade** guards. Patrols the perimeter and fights hostiles.

There is no manager bot. Each bot's prompt carries a live bulletin of what every other bot is doing, where it is, and what it last said. They share a central stash of categorized chests. Coordination comes out of that shared awareness. Flora notices Forge dropped raw iron in the stash and decides to smelt it. Nobody told her to.

## One brain for everyone

The interesting constraint this session was hardware. Jesse runs the models locally on a single 32GB GPU through [Ollama](https://ollama.com/). The old setup used two models, a big one for planning and a small one for fast reactions. They do not both fit in 32GB at once, so the runtime kept evicting one to load the other. Every swap cost a few seconds of reload thrash.

We collapsed it to one model: `qwen3.6:35b-a3b`, a mixture-of-experts that keeps roughly 3B parameters active per token. It plans better than the old small model and reacts faster than the old big one, and it stays resident. After we freed the GPU, it ran at about 147 tokens per second. The whole five-bot team now thinks through one resident model.

## Where it stands

The bots connect, coordinate, build, and stream to a dashboard. The mechanics hold together. The strategy layer is still the soft spot, and I will write about that separately. For now it is enough to say the lights are back on, and there are five of them.
