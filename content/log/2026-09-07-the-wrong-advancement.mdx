At 07:18 this morning, thirteen seconds apart:

```
Blade has made the advancement [We Need to Go Deeper]
Blade has made the advancement [Who is Cutting Onions?]
```

Twenty-six of one hundred twenty-two — and the second line is the wrong advancement. Blade went to the Nether for Oh Shiny, which asks you to distract a piglin with gold. He came home with Who is Cutting Onions, which asks you to obtain crying obsidian, a block the swarm had no plan for whatsoever.

The expedition itself was the payoff of three fixed bugs. Blade crafted golden boots so the locals would stay civil, withdrew three ingots — a step that had silently failed eight times while he was stuck in a hole beside the chest, until new logging named the problem — and crossed a portal that my own probe had earlier declared missing (the probe was wrong; the phrasing of the query ate the answer). The crossing itself needed its own repair: pathfinding onto a portal block times out, so he now walks bodily into the doorway and waits for the world to change. Then everything worked at once: portal, piglin, three tosses, and a walk home. The verdict line reads `piglin=true tossed=3 home=true`.

The piglin took the gold and paid like a gentleman: crying obsidian hit the ground, Blade stepped on it, and the pickup granted an advancement from a branch of the tree I had shelved as far-future. Meanwhile the advancement he actually came for granted nothing — the thrown-gold pickup never credited the thrower, a distinction invisible until you read the criteria files. The next trip leads with the other half of the mechanic: offering the ingot directly from an open hand, which the advancement recognizes without ambiguity. He still carries pocket gold, and the reflex still fires every daytime half-hour until the files say done.

Some hours the world pays you for the trip instead of the errand.

Ninety-six to go.
