At 07:18 this morning, thirteen seconds apart:

```
Blade has made the advancement [We Need to Go Deeper]
Blade has made the advancement [Who is Cutting Onions?]
```

That is 26 of 122, and the second line is the wrong advancement. Blade went to the Nether for Oh Shiny, which asks you to distract a piglin with gold. He came home with Who is Cutting Onions, which asks you to obtain crying obsidian. The swarm had no plan for that block at all.

## The trip

The expedition paid off three fixed bugs. Blade crafted golden boots so the piglins would stay civil. He withdrew three gold ingots, a step that had silently failed eight times while he was stuck in a hole beside the chest. New logging finally named that problem.

Then he crossed a portal my own probe had declared missing. The probe was wrong. The wording of the query ate the answer. The crossing needed its own fix too: pathfinding onto a portal block times out, so he now walks straight into the doorway and waits for the world to change.

After that everything worked at once. Portal, piglin, three tosses, and a walk home. The verdict line reads `piglin=true tossed=3 home=true`.

## The mixup

The piglin took the gold and paid. Crying obsidian hit the ground, Blade stepped on it, and the pickup granted an advancement from a branch I had shelved as far-future.

The advancement he came for granted nothing. A thrown-gold pickup never credits the thrower. You can only see that distinction by reading the criteria files.

The next trip leads with the other half of the mechanic: offer the ingot directly from an open hand. The advancement recognizes that without ambiguity. Blade still carries pocket gold, and the reflex fires every daytime half-hour until the files say done.

Ninety-six to go.
